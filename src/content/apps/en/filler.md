A puzzle of space, played with one finger. Press and hold to grow a ball, and let go before it touches anything.

Every ball you place claims a piece of the field. Fill the field to finish the level, and spend as few balls as you can: the ones you never use become your stars.

### HOW IT PLAYS

- Press and hold in open space. The ball grows while you hold it.
- Let go before it touches a wall, another ball or a hazard.
- A ball touched while it grows pops and is gone.
- A press on something already there costs you a ball and grows nothing.
- Fill the field to 100% to finish the level.

### WHAT GETS IN THE WAY

- Drifting hazards cross the field at a steady pace.
- Fast ones leave less room than you think.
- Erratic ones change direction on every bounce.

### THREE CHAPTERS

- An open field first: one, two or three colours, each filling its share in turn.
- Then coloured areas, where only their own colour may start a ball inside.
- Then areas that move, drifting and closing as the round runs on.
- 270 levels in all, and 270 challenges beside them.

### CHALLENGES

- One ball, one target: grow a single ball to the size the round asks for.
- Packing rounds: a handful of balls, and more than a full field to cover.
- Opened by the stars your levels earn, and worth no stars themselves.

### STARS

- Three stars for finishing with balls to spare, one for finishing at all.
- Stars open the challenges, and 60% of a chapter's stars open the next chapter.

### LEARNING IT

- A walkthrough plays over a round of its own and points at what matters.
- How to play is a page per rule, open from the title screen whenever you want it.
- Every level says what it is asking for, in one line.

### ALSO

- Five languages: English, French, Spanish, Hindi and Chinese.
- Sound and vibration switch on or off without leaving the round.
- Lose a level and an ad will hand back a full stock. Never required, never asked for twice.

No timer. No lives. No streak to lose.
