---
title: "Vízállás"
description: "Water levels of rivers and lakes in Hungary."
language: "en-US"
---

With the Vízállás app, you can quickly and easily monitor the current water levels of Hungary’s rivers and lakes. Based on official hydrological data, the app presents the water levels of the country’s most important rivers and lakes in a clear, easy-to-use interface.

It can be a useful companion for anglers, hikers, people living near the water, and anyone who wants to keep track of current water-level conditions. Our goal is to make reliable water-level data available in one place, quickly and without unnecessary searching.
