## 1. About the app

The Filler is a mobile game in which players grow and drop balls to fill a playfield while avoiding moving hazards. It is played across three chapters of levels and challenges.

The app does not require an account, registration, name, phone number, or email address. The app does not operate its own user-account or application backend. The app does not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library data. The app does not include in-app purchases or subscription features.

## 2. Data stored locally

The app may store the following information locally on the device:

- progress: stars earned for each level and the challenges completed, in each chapter;
- sound, vibration, and level-briefing settings;
- which level briefings have already been shown, and whether the tutorial has been seen;
- the last position of each level and challenge list.

This information is not sent to my own server. Local data can be removed by deleting the app or clearing its app data.

The device's language setting is read when the app starts, so that the game can be shown in that language where a translation exists. It is not stored by the app and is not sent anywhere.

## 3. Google AdMob advertising

The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve, measure, and protect advertising services, Google and its advertising partners may process technical information such as:

- IP address and approximate regional information;
- device and operating-system information;
- technical or advertising identifiers;
- ad impressions and ad interactions;
- app version and technical operating environment.

Whether personalized advertising is used depends on applicable consent and opt-out settings.

More information:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google AdMob](https://support.google.com/admob/)

## 4. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

The app uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage privacy choices related to advertising.

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, UMP may display the required privacy-consent messages. Users in applicable US states can manage available opt-out choices through the app’s privacy settings.

Privacy settings are available from **Info / Privacy options** in the app when UMP determines that a privacy-options entry point is required.

## 5. Firebase Crashlytics

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect, investigate, and fix crashes and other technical problems.

Crashlytics may process, among other things:

- crash reports and stack traces;
- app version and package identifier;
- operating-system and device information;
- Crashlytics and Firebase installation identifiers;
- the time and technical context of a crash, including which chapter and which level or challenge was being played.

The app does not directly request a name, phone number, or email address.

More information is available in the [Firebase Privacy and Security documentation](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

Google and the other third-party service providers used by the app provide data protection that is the same as or equivalent to the protection described in this Privacy Policy, as applicable to their services.

## 6. IDFA and App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, the app asks for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before the advertising identifier (IDFA) may be used. The request appears once advertising is being set up, and the answer can be changed at any time in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If permission is given, advertising may use the identifier to personalise the ads shown. If it is refused — or if tracking requests are turned off for all apps — advertising still appears, but is not personalised, and the identifier is not used.

Android has no equivalent prompt. There the advertising identifier is governed by the consent choices described above, and it can be reset or deleted in Android Settings under Privacy → Ads.

## 7. Data retention

Information stored locally by the app remains on the device until the user deletes it, removes the app, or clears the app’s data.

Google AdMob and Firebase Crashlytics process and retain information according to their own privacy, security, and retention policies. According to Firebase, Crashlytics retains certain crash and identifier data for 90 days.

## 8. Data security

Network communication uses secure protocols supported by the relevant services. The relevant data controllers apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data.
