## 1. About this policy

This policy covers the lucivum.github.io website only: its pages about my apps, their privacy policies and my contact details. Each app has its own privacy policy, linked from that app's page.

The website has no user accounts, forms or comments, and does not ask for your name or email address.

## 2. Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you open a page, GitHub processes technical information needed to deliver it and keep the service secure, such as your IP address, browser information, the page requested and the time of the request. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in running a secure and working website.

More information: [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## 3. Cookies and Google Analytics

With your consent only, the website loads Google Analytics, which uses cookies to measure visits, the pages viewed, and clicks on the Google Play buttons. When you click a Google Play button, the app and the page language are recorded.

To provide this, Google may process information such as:

- IP address and approximate location;
- browser and device information;
- cookie identifiers;
- the pages visited and the interactions on them.

Until you accept, it is not loaded and no request is made to Google. Rejecting does not limit the website: every page works the same either way.

The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

More information:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

The website does not use Google Analytics data for advertising, and the advertising features of Google's tag are turned off.

## 4. Your cookie choice

Your choice is stored in your own browser (local storage, under the name `lucivum-consent`) so the banner does not ask again on every page. It is not sent to me or to anyone else.

You can change your choice at any time with **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page, or remove it by clearing this website's data in your browser.

## 5. Links to Google Play

The Google Play buttons lead to Google Play, where Google's own privacy policy applies.

## 6. Data transfers

GitHub and Google may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their own privacy policies.

## 7. Data retention

GitHub and Google keep the data they process according to their own privacy and retention policies. Your cookie choice stays in your browser until you clear it.

## 8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to personal data concerning you, correction or deletion of data, restriction of processing, objection to processing, withdrawal of consent, and information about data processing.

Requests may be sent using the controller contact details listed above. Complaints may be submitted to the relevant data-protection authority in your country of residence or location; in Hungary this is the NAIH.

## 9. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy when the website changes how it handles data. The latest version will show a new “Last updated” date.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the controller contact details listed above.
